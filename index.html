JRTC Wargame for Apple Vision Pro

Shared terrain planning and after-action review

I co-built JRTC Wargame with AI to give planners a shared spatial view of terrain, force posture, and courses of action. The visionOS app includes an offline JRTC training area, tactical overlays, bounded simulation, COA comparison, and repeatable after-action review.

Global Logistics Drone Simulator

Locally run sustainment analysis

I co-built Global Logistics Drone Simulator with AI to compare sustainment capacity, routes, and unmanned lift in replayable local scenarios. It runs locally, and its Apple Vision Pro companion displays those offline scenarios on a 3D globe. The activity shown is modeled data, not a live operational feed.

05

USNS Montford Point

Notional maritime logistics visualization

I co-built this Blender concept with AI to explore how a ballasted expeditionary transfer dock could stage and release uncrewed surface craft for distributed food resupply. The package pairs a public-reference ship exterior with a fictional, non-weapon loadout, a repeatable 60-second deployment sequence, and an interactive web model.

Notional visualization only. It is not engineering CAD, a documented ship configuration, or an operational proposal.

  • Blender
  • 60-second film
  • Interactive GLB

Retirement Hunter

FIRE and retirement planning in one place

I co-built Retirement Hunter with AI to put retirement planning in one place and make a practical question—“Can I retire?”—easier to explore. It is an educational planning tool that brings together FIRE progress, Monte Carlo analysis, tax and Roth strategy, Social Security, withdrawals, healthcare, milestones, and net-worth tracking.

About

I apply operations research to real operational problems.

I’m a U.S. Army officer and operations research graduate student at the Naval Postgraduate School. I graduated from the United States Merchant Marine Academy with a B.S. in Marine Engineering Systems and commissioned as a Military Intelligence officer in 2014. My assignments include 1-509 IN (ABN) at JRTC, Fort Polk, and the 264th Combat Sustainment Support Battalion, 3rd Expeditionary Sustainment Command. I deployed in support of Operation Spartan Shield and Operation Inherent Resolve.

I’m from Virginia and grew up a military brat. I’m married, and my wife and I have a son named Andre.
