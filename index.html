Starfall Defender

Browser-native tactical 3D space combat

Co-built with AI, Starfall Defender tests how far browser-native 3D combat can go while preserving tactical clarity, responsive controls, and accessibility. The result pairs the SF-19 Kestrel’s defense of Earth with a guided 12-lesson flight school.

02

JRTC Wargame for Apple Vision Pro

Shared terrain, simulation, and after-action review

Co-built with AI, JRTC Wargame turns terrain, force posture, and course-of-action analysis into a shared spatial workspace. The visionOS system combines an offline JRTC training area, tactical overlays, bounded simulation, COA comparison, and deterministic after-action review.

  • visionOS
  • RealityKit
  • Geospatial analysis
03

Drone Delivery Simulator

Last-mile fleet and charging analysis

Co-built with MAJ Joshua Brewster and AI, Drone Delivery Simulator makes fleet size, charging capacity, demand, restricted areas, and routing assumptions visible before a planner commits resources. The native desktop model has a browser twin published through Army Vantage for authorized use.

04

Global Logistics Drone Simulator

Local, auditable sustainment analysis

Co-built with AI, Global Logistics Drone Simulator helps planners compare capacity, routing, and unmanned-lift choices through replayable local scenarios. Its Apple Vision Pro companion projects verified offline scenario packages onto a measured-reference globe; the displayed activity is modeled data, not a live operational feed.

Runs locally

  • Local-first
  • Simulation audit
  • Apple Vision Pro
05

Retirement Hunter

Integrated FIRE and retirement planning

Co-built with AI, Retirement Hunter makes “Can I retire?” easier to explore in one connected workspace instead of scattered spreadsheets. It brings together FIRE progress, Monte Carlo analysis, tax and Roth strategy, Social Security, withdrawals, healthcare, milestones, and net-worth tracking.

About

Operations research, built for the field.

MAJ Brandon De La Rosa is a U.S. Army officer and Operations Research graduate student at the Naval Postgraduate School. A graduate of the United States Merchant Marine Academy (B.S., Marine Engineering Systems), he commissioned as a Military Intelligence Officer in 2014. His assignments include 1-509 IN (ABN) at JRTC Fort Polk and the 264th Combat Sustainment Support Battalion, 3rd Expeditionary Sustainment Command, with deployments supporting Operation Spartan Shield and Operation Inherent Resolve.

From Virginia. Military brat. Married with one young child.
